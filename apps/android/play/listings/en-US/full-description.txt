Travelmo keeps every trip in one place. Connect Gmail on the Travelmo website and your flight, train, hotel and car bookings arrive by themselves, grouped into trips by date. You don't type anything in.

Your trips
• See what's happening now, what's next and where you've been.
• Each trip lists its bookings in order, with times shown as written for the place they happen.
• Add, edit or delete a booking by hand when you need to.

Share with the people you travel with
• Share a trip by email. They see the same bookings and can add their own.

Stay ready
• Get a reminder before a trip, by email or as a notification on your phone.
• Travelmo warns you when a passport or visa runs out too soon, and says what each country asks of your passport.

Look back
• The calendar shows your travel month by month.
• Reports count your trips, days away and countries on a world map, year by year.

More ways in
• Import your TripIt export, calendar files and calendar feeds, or forward confirmations from any mail service.

Travelmo uses your email only to find bookings and add them to your trips.
