# Brand logos

The SVG files here are logos of the brands they are named for (by the id in
`packages/core/src/brands.ts`): from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org)
16.38.1 (CC0) and [SVG Logos](https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos) (CC0)
in their brand colours, and from [Arcticons](https://github.com/Arcticons-Team/Arcticons)
(CC BY-SA 4.0, by Donnnno and contributors) as line icons, recoloured, which
these files share under the same licence. The marks are trademarks of
their owners, shown only to identify each brand beside its booking. Android
bundles the same icons as PNGs (`composeResources/drawable/brand_*.png`).
