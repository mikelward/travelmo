# Airline logos

The SVG files here are the icons of the airlines they are named for (by IATA
code), from [Soaring Symbols](https://github.com/soaring-symbols/soaring-symbols)
0.6.1. They are trademarks of those airlines, not licensed by that project or
this one, and are shown only to identify each airline beside its name. Android
bundles the same icons as PNGs (`apps/android/shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/drawable/airline_*.png`).
